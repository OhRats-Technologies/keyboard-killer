Install
Gaze + voice for the Linux desktop

Look. Speak. It clicks.

Look at a target, say what you mean, and Keyboard-Killer does the rest. Stereo cameras and local speech recognition stay on your machine.

Linux v0.1.0 · x86_64 · Debian/Ubuntu or AppImage
Script curl -fsSL https://keyboard-killer.ohrats.party/install.sh | sh
Package curl -fLo /tmp/keyboard-killer.deb https://keyboard-killer.ohrats.party/releases/keyboard-killer_amd64.deb && sudo apt install /tmp/keyboard-killer.deb
AppImage chmod +x Keyboard-Killer-*.AppImage && ./Keyboard-Killer-*.AppImage

The .deb includes the /dev/uinput udev rule, a desktop entry, and an optional systemd user unit. Two cameras are required for stereo gaze; the app reports a single camera as degraded.

macOS is not available yet.

The macOS host is a capability stub in this release: CGEvent, AVFoundation, ScreenCaptureKit, TCC, and notarization are not implemented. No installer is published.

01 / What it does

Hands on the desktop, not on the mouse.

01

Look, then speak

Two stable cameras build a stereo gaze estimate. Say “click send” and the click lands where you were looking.

02

Local by default

Parakeet speech recognition runs on your CPU. Camera, speech, and history stay on the machine.

03

Bring your own AI

Optional OpenAI-compatible, Ollama, or ACP agent providers. Deterministic pointer and voice paths never depend on one.

04

Recover in plain language

doctor and setup report cameras, audio, permissions, models, and providers with one next action.

02 / Privacy

Your camera and voice stay here.

Screenshots only leave the device if you explicitly enable a remote gaze VLM. API keys live in the OS keychain.
